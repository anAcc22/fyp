LatinModernMonoLight10-Bold.ttf is Latin Modern Mono Light 10 Bold from the GUST e-foundry, with its outlines converted
from OpenType to TrueType so that matplotlib can embed it. It is the same typewriter design as the code command in the
paper, but heavier, as the regular weight looks thin in figures. It is under the GUST Font License:
https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/licenses
